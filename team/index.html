Meet the Team

Learn from experienced students who have succeeded at the highest levels of competitive math and coding.

Louis Junghyo Yoo

Founder

Louis Junghyo Yoo is a rising sophomore at Lake Washington High School and a student leader dedicated to mathematics and computer science. His quantitative and technical skills are demonstrated through a strong competitive track record. He holds his school's highest AMC 10 score among underclassmen. He also competes in the USACO Silver division and has an award-winning background in international robotics. Ultimately, he aims to use his talents to bridge the STEM opportunity gap, ensuring computer science resources are accessible to everyone.

Anish Agrawal

Co-Founder

Anish Agrawal engineers software across multiple robotics teams, building autonomous systems, custom odometry localization, and kinematic pathfinding engines. He has qualified for the USA Junior Mathematical Olympiad and USACO Gold, alongside delivering high-level performances in robotics tournaments. Passionate about tackling complex coding and math challenges, he continually works through personal projects to advance his thinking. Beyond his own work, Anish gives back to STEM education as a competitive math tutor and problem writer.

John Choe

Co-Founder

John Choe is a junior at Bellevue High School with a strong passion for competitive mathematics, computer science, and programming. He is an AIME qualifier, a USACO Silver Division competitor, and an ACSL Senior Division Bronze Award recipient. John also earned first place in his grade in the NMSC Seattle Chapter Mathematics Competition for six consecutive years, holds both the PCAP and PCEP Python certifications. Passionate about mentoring younger students, he enjoys helping them develop problem-solving skills and confidence through coding.
